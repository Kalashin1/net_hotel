Common Questions

Frequently Asked

What are your check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 15:00, and check-out is by 12:00. Early check-in and late check-out can be arranged subject to availability — please contact our reception in advance. Suite guests enjoy flexible timing as part of their dedicated service.

Do you offer airport transfers?

Yes. We offer complimentary luxury transfers for suite guests. For other accommodations, private transfers are available in a range of vehicles from Mercedes sedans to vans for families. Helicopter transfers from Nice Heliport can also be arranged. Please contact our concierge at least 48 hours before arrival.

Is parking available at the hotel?

Complimentary underground valet parking is available for all guests. We also offer electric vehicle charging stations, and can arrange car rental, chauffeur services, or luxury vehicle hire during your stay.

Are pets welcome at The Aurelia?

We welcome well-behaved pets in selected rooms and Garden Villas. A pet amenity package including bed, bowls, and treats is provided. Please inform us at the time of booking so we can prepare the perfect accommodation for your furry companion.

What is your cancellation policy?

Our standard flexible rate allows free cancellation up to 48 hours before arrival. Special offers, packages, and peak-season bookings may have different terms. La Cantina private dining requires 7 days notice. Please review your specific booking confirmation or contact reservations for details.

Do you accommodate dietary requirements?

Absolutely. Our chefs craft dedicated menus for vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, kosher, halal, and allergy-specific requirements. Please inform us at the time of booking or speak with our maître d' upon arrival — every guest's needs are treated with the utmost care.

Can you arrange weddings or events?

The Aurelia is a celebrated venue for intimate weddings, milestone celebrations, and corporate events. Our dedicated events team can host gatherings from 12 to 200 guests across multiple venues — from the cliffside terrace to La Cantina wine cellar. Contact events@theaurelia.com to begin planning.
