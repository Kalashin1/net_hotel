A Sample Day

From Sunrise to Stars

A glimpse into a possible day at The Aurelia — though every moment is tailored to your desires.

06:30 — Sunrise

Sunrise Yoga on the Cliffs

Begin the day with a private yoga session on our cliffside terrace as the sun paints the Mediterranean in gold. Our resident instructor guides you through a flow designed to awaken body and spirit.

08:00 — Breakfast

Breakfast at Osteria del Mare

A leisurely breakfast on the terrace — fresh pastries from our bakery, eggs from local farms, seasonal fruits, and our signature Aurelia granola. Or enjoy it in the privacy of your suite.

10:00 — Morning

Coastal Hiking or Yacht Charter

Choose your adventure: hike the ancient coastal trails with a naturalist guide, or board our yacht for a day of hidden coves and snorkeling. The Mediterranean reveals itself differently to every guest.

13:00 — Afternoon

Spa Ritual & Lunch at Lido

Surrender to a 90-minute Aurelia Signature Ritual in the spa, then stroll to Lido Beach Club for a barefoot lunch of grilled fish and local wine — toes in the sand, horizon in view.

16:00 — Late Afternoon

Old Town Discovery

A private guided walk through the medieval old town — hidden piazzas, artisan workshops, and a gelato stop at the 100-year-old shop locals love. Return by vintage Fiat convertible if you wish.

19:30 — Evening

Dinner at Osteria del Mare

The seven-course Mare Nostrum tasting menu, paired with rare wines from our cellar. Chef Bellini's daily composition — a meditation on the sea and soil of this coast.

22:00 — Night

Cocktails at Terrazza

End the evening with a signature cocktail at the rooftop bar, under a canopy of stars. Live jazz on Fridays, or simply the sound of the waves far below.
