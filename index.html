Welcome to The Aurelia

A Legacy of Timeless Hospitality

Perched on the sun-kissed cliffs of the Mediterranean, The Aurelia has welcomed discerning travelers since 1987. What began as a family-owned villa has blossomed into one of Europe's most cherished coastal sanctuaries — a place where heritage meets contemporary luxury.

Every corner of our estate tells a story: hand-laid marble from Carrara, linens woven in Florence, and a culinary philosophy rooted in the rhythms of the sea and soil. Here, time slows down, and every moment is crafted with intention.

Isabella Moretti
